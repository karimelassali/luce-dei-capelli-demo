# Luce dei Capelli — Design Direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Terracotta Atelier
Very Brief Intro: A warm editorial salon identity built around tactile materials, soft light, and confident typography. It should feel local, personal, and quietly premium rather than clinical or trend-chasing.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Citrus Studio
Very Brief Intro: A bright, playful beauty studio language using citrus accents, cropped imagery, and energetic movement. It would feel youthful and social-first.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Midnight Gloss
Very Brief Intro: A dark, cinematic salon identity with glossy black surfaces, warm metallic details, and high-contrast portraiture. It would feel dramatic and fashion-led.
Probability: 0.05

## Chosen direction: Terracotta Atelier

### Design Movement
Contemporary Italian editorial design blended with Mediterranean materiality and modernist salon signage.

### Core Principles
1. Treat hair as a tactile material: curves, gloss, strands, and warm light lead the visual language.
2. Use generous asymmetry and editorial pacing rather than a generic centered marketing grid.
3. Keep the interface intimate and useful: the booking handoff, address, hours, and services must always be easy to reach.
4. Make every surface feel considered through paper-like cream backgrounds, terracotta accents, espresso ink, and subtle grain.

### Color Philosophy
The palette uses sun-warmed terracotta as the ownable signature color, balanced by chalk cream and espresso brown. Terracotta signals craft, warmth, and Mediterranean locality; cream gives the content breathing room; espresso creates the contrast needed for readable CTAs and practical details. The palette should feel like plaster, linen, wood, and polished hair rather than generic beauty pink.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an offset editorial composition: a split hero with the portrait on one side and copy on the other, then alternating bands where imagery and content shift sides. Practical contact actions are anchored in a slim floating rail on desktop and a compact bottom bar on mobile.

### Signature Elements
1. A terracotta sun-disc motif used as a section marker and background accent.
2. Fine hairline rules and oversized italic serif numerals to mark service categories.
3. Rounded image windows that echo the silhouette of a hair wave without overusing cards.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like a gentle handoff, not a dashboard. Buttons have tactile press states, image tiles lift slightly on hover, and contact actions remain persistent without becoming intrusive. The main CTA always routes to the salon's preferred booking/contact channel, while the demo clearly labels the handoff as a placeholder until the owner confirms the channel.

### Animation
Use slow editorial reveals for the hero image and section headings, followed by 30–60ms staggered service rows. Keep hover motion to transform and opacity only, under 240ms. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No looping decorative animation should compete with the portfolio imagery.

### Typography System
Display: Cormorant Garamond, with italic weight used sparingly for expressive phrases and oversized numerals. Body/UI: DM Sans, with medium and semibold weights for practical clarity. Headlines should be sentence case with tight line-height; labels use small uppercase tracking; service names use a readable serif/sans pairing.

### Brand Essence
Luce dei Capelli is a warm, independent hair studio for people in Castel San Giovanni who want considered cuts, color, and styling with a personal touch. Personality: luminous, tactile, unhurried.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are assured and evocative without being vague. CTAs are direct and human. Microcopy is short, useful, and local.

Example lines:
- “Your hair, in its best light.”
- “Tell us what you’re imagining — we’ll find the right appointment.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied abstract flowing-ribbon mark beside a restrained wordmark set in spaced uppercase sans, with “LUCE” as the visual anchor and “DEI CAPELLI” as the small supporting line. The mark should be visible in the header and as the favicon; do not reduce it to an unreadable tiny icon.

### Signature Brand Color
Terracotta Ember: #B95845.

## Style Decisions
- Use generated salon photography as the primary visual anchor rather than generic stock imagery.
- Avoid purple gradients, default rounded-card layouts, and Inter.
- Keep the primary CTA visible within the first viewport and repeat it only at meaningful decision points.

## Demo assumptions
- Publicly verified facts from the report are limited to the business name, category, address, and social/appointment-oriented presence. Opening hours, service names, prices, logo approval, and preferred booking channel are presented as demo placeholders until confirmed by the owner.
- The demo uses a clear “demo / details to confirm” note where operational facts are not yet verified.
